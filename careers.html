Appointment Setter

  • Fully remote
  • Commission only
  • $50 per completed appointment
View detailsHide details ▼

You’ll be the first conversation a business has with Vo Creative Co. Through DMs and cold email, you’ll start real conversations with business owners, find the ones who are a good fit, and book them onto calls with our team. You get paid for every completed appointment, and more when those appointments turn into clients.

What you’ll do

  • Run DM outreach to business owners
  • Send cold email outreach
  • Follow up with leads who respond
  • Qualify leads and figure out what they need
  • Book qualified leads onto calls with our team
  • Keep your lead notes organized

What you need

  • Confident, clear writing in DMs and email
  • Comfort talking to business owners
  • Organized and reliable with follow ups
  • Sales or appointment setting experience is a plus

How you get paid

  • Per appointment$50 for every completed appointment
  • Closed client+$150 for every appointment that becomes a client
  • Bonus+$100 when 3 of your appointments close
  • StructureCommission only
  • LocationFully remote

How to apply

  • Send us your past experience
  • Record a short video telling us why you’re the best candidate for the job
