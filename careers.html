Appointment Setter

  • Part-time
  • Remote
  • Training provided
View detailsHide details ▼

You’ll be the first conversation a business has with Vo Creative Co. You’ll reach out to leads, start real conversations, qualify who’s a good fit, and book them onto calls with our team. If you’re confident on the phone and in the DMs, this one is for you.

What you’ll do

  • Reach out to leads by phone, text, email, and DM
  • Follow up with inbound inquiries quickly
  • Qualify leads and figure out what they need
  • Book qualified leads onto calls with our team
  • Keep the calendar and lead notes organized
  • Represent the studio in a professional, friendly way

What you need

  • Confident, clear communication
  • Comfort talking to business owners
  • Organized and reliable with follow ups
  • Sales or appointment setting experience is a plus
