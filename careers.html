Social Media Manager

  • Part-time
  • $25 / hour
  • Travel required

You’ll be on the ground at client shoots — capturing every planned video, directing on the fly, and turning a shoot plan into finished, organized footage. Gas is covered, gear is provided, and we train you before you ever run a shoot on your own.

What you’ll do

  • Drive to scheduled shoots
  • Execute on the shoot plan
  • Film every planned video
  • Talk to clients in a professional manner
  • Direct and think on the fly
  • Use the gear effectively
  • Communicate problems efficiently
  • After the shoot, organize clips based on the video plan
  • Give detail on the thinking behind each video idea

What you need

  • Experience in social media or videography
  • Ability to travel to scheduled shoots
  • Professional, client-ready communication
  • The ability to direct and problem-solve on the fly

The details

  • Pay$25 / hour
  • SchedulePart-time
  • TrainingRequired — a one-week training process before work begins
  • TravelRequired; gas expenses are completely covered
  • GearAll camera gear and equipment is provided
