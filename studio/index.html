The studio, drawn for founders.

2,000 square feet of warehouse in downtown Detroit. Every room has one job: help you grow. Tap a room to see what happens there.

Concept plan
Rev 0 · Nov 2026

Room schedule

  1. An open white cyc for photo and video. Shoot your product, your founder story and your next launch. A roll-up door off the alley brings gear straight in, and on launch nights it becomes the event room.

  2. A camera-ready kitchen with an island. Film recipes, product demos and lifestyle content, then use it to cater the after-party.

  3. Mirrors, wardrobe and a quiet minute before you go on camera. It opens straight onto both sets, so talent never crosses the lobby.

  4. Treated walls, four mics and cameras on every seat. Record your show, your ads and a month of clips in one sitting.

  5. Where the best introductions happen. Open to the entry and the lounge, so there’s always somewhere to start a conversation.

  6. Workshops on marketing, AI and branding for about 20 founders at a time. Between sessions it’s where the community works side by side. A sliding wall opens it to the white space for big nights.

  7. Check in, grab a coffee, find your people. The front desk sits right inside the street doors.

  8. Lights, stands and backdrops on shelves, plus the back-of-house hall to the green room and both sets.

  9. A single accessible restroom off the entry.
