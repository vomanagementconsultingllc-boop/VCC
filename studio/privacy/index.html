Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

This policy explains what information Vo Creative Studio (“we”, “us”) collects through this website, our waitlist, our emails and our text messages, how we use it, and the choices you have. Vo Creative Studio is operated by VO Management Consulting LLC, based in Detroit, Michigan.

1. What we collect

Information you give us. When you join the waitlist we collect your name, email address and phone number. If you tick the marketing box, we also record that you agreed, the exact wording you agreed to and when you agreed.

Information collected automatically. We record the page or link that brought you to us, such as a referring website or campaign tags in the link you clicked. Our website host keeps standard server logs (for example IP address, browser type and the time of your visit) to run and secure the site. Our fonts are delivered by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to send them. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on these pages.

2. How we use it

  • To add you to the waitlist, save your founding-member pricing and invite you to our private founders community on Discord.
  • To send you launch news, founding-member pricing and offers by email and text message, only if you opted in.
  • To answer your questions and provide the services you sign up for.
  • To keep records of your consent, prevent spam and abuse, and meet our legal obligations.

4. How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run the waitlist, such as our website and form host (Netlify) and the providers we use to send emails and text messages. They may use your information only to provide those services to us.
  • With Discord, if you choose to join our community there. Discord’s own privacy policy applies to your use of Discord.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our members, the public or us.
  • If our business changes hands, for example through a merger or sale, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you’re on the waitlist or a member, and afterwards only as long as we need to for legal and record-keeping reasons, such as proof of your consent to receive messages. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

6. Your choices

  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email.
  • Texts: reply STOP to any text message.
  • Your information: email us at nathan@vocreativeco.com to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, and we will honor them.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Vo Creative Studio is built for founders and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the studio grows. We’ll change the effective date above, and if the changes are significant we’ll let people on our list know by email.
